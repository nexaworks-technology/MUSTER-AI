## 1. Goal
Cut meeting prep to minutes and turn calls into account data. Draft QBRs from locked real numbers. Share success plans and reviews through a scoped customer portal. Run simple surveys that feed health with proof.

## 2. In scope
- **Models** ([W], RLS + composite FKs): `Meeting`, `MeetingAttendee`, `CallTranscript` (if not yet created), `SuccessPlan`, `Goal` (complete), `Document`, `Survey`, `SurveyResponse`, `PortalAccess`.
- **Adapters:**
  - `google-calendar` and `microsoft-calendar` → `NormalizedMeeting`. Accounts are matched by attendee email/domain, reusing the M9 matching service; unmatched meetings are never attached to an account.
  - `fireflies`, `gong`, `zoom` → `NormalizedTranscript`.
  - Manual transcript upload (vtt/srt/txt/json) through `src/lib/storage` with upload safety. Nothing is processed until `FileObject.scanStatus = CLEAN` (Q-18).
- **Pre-call brief.**
  - Job `meetings.prepareBrief` runs at T-30 min and on demand, idempotent per `(meetingId, briefVersion)`.
  - It creates a `Document` of kind PRE_CALL_BRIEF: health + reasons (links), last conversations, open tickets and promises, renewal, suggested questions.
  - Agent `meeting-qbr` with `prompts/meeting-brief.md`, tier `standard`.
- **Call summaries.** When a transcript arrives, `meeting-qbr` produces a summary, decisions and action items (→ `Task` with owners) via `prompts/meeting-summary.md`. Risks, goals and promises flow through the M9 Extraction pipeline (no parallel path).
- **QBR / business review** (`/meetings/qbr/new`):
  1. Pick an account and period. Deterministic code computes the metrics into `Document.metrics` (locked JSON): adoption, health by pillar, support experience, goals vs results, renewal/commercial (only with `canViewRevenue`).
  2. `meeting-qbr` (tier `deep`, `prompts/qbr-narrative.md`) writes narrative blocks that use only `{{metric.key}}` placeholders for numbers.
  3. The **placeholder validator** rejects any narrative containing digits outside placeholders (allowlisted: dates and period labels) or referencing unknown metric keys.
  4. Charts are rendered only for metrics that have data, and a "No data for this period" label shows otherwise.
  5. A human edits the narrative; the numbers are not editable. Export to PDF goes through the storage module.
- **Success Plans** (`/success-plans`, Account 360 tab): goals, milestones, progress, `sharedWithPortal` toggle (audited).
- **Customer Portal** (`/portal/*`):
  - Magic-link `PortalAccess` per (account, contact). The token is stored hashed and expires (default 14 days, config). Revoke and rate limit are both enforced.
  - Shows the plan, goals, progress, shared documents and reviews.
  - **Q-22 default (conservative):** read-only, except the contact can mark progress on their own customer-side steps/goals and leave comments. Every portal write is audited with `actorType = PORTAL_CONTACT`.
  - Confirm against `docs/QUESTIONS.md` Q-22 before building any wider interactivity.
- **Surveys** (`/settings/surveys`):
  - Types: NPS / CSAT / CES.
  - Journey-moment triggers: onboarding complete (`onboarding.valueReached`), ticket closed, 60 days before renewal.
  - Sending goes through `outbound.dispatch` with guards and approval levels; nothing bypasses Rule 3. Responses arrive via a tokenized link.
  - Responses feed the Sentiment pillar (CSAT/NPS component) and the Relationship pillar (NPS from decision makers), each with a `HealthReason` whose sources point to the `SurveyResponse` id.
  - The `survey.responded` event can trigger plays.
- **Plays.** Waking the three dormant plays:
  - `engagement-loss` (triggered on the meeting/engagement data M10 builds)
  - `qbr-due`
  - `business-review-follow-up`
  For each play, "awake" means: (1) detector exists and triggers honestly, (2) safety check passes pre-publish, and (3) dormant label is updated/cleared.
- **Today.** The Meetings section is populated with "brief ready" links.

## 4. Build steps (dependency order; each slice ends with `pnpm gate` green)
1. **Schema slice.** All M10 models + migration (RLS, composite FKs, and the CHECK `origin <> 'AI' OR agent_run_id IS NOT NULL` on Document). Extend the tenant suite.
2. **Placeholder validator (test-first)** in `src/modules/success-plans/qbr/placeholders.ts`.
3. **QBR metrics computation (test-first)** in `src/modules/success-plans/qbr/metrics.ts`. Pure functions over records; no LLM.
4. **Calendar adapters slice.** Contract tests, meeting ingest, attendee matching, Today meetings section.
   - Once meeting data exists, switch on M3's dormant `no_meeting_in_x_days` rule (it becomes enable-able in Settings › Signals; it still never fires on an account with no meeting source) and feed Engagement's meeting component, which M3 left `null` (`docs/REVIEW-2026-11-03.md` S3).
5. **Pre-call brief slice.** Job, agent, prompt, idempotency, brief view, scheduler at T-30.
6. **Transcript slice.**
   - Fireflies/Gong/Zoom adapters with contract tests.
   - Manual upload with scan gating.
   - Summary agent; action items → Tasks; Extraction pipeline handoff.
7. **QBR slice.** Generate → locked metrics → narrative → validator → editor (narrative only) → PDF export → share to portal.
8. **Success Plans slice.** CRUD, goals and milestones, progress, share toggle.
9. **Portal slice.**
   - Magic-link issue/revoke (Admin/CSM).
   - A portal session scoped to (workspaceId, accountId, contactId); every query is built from the PortalAccess row, never from URL params.
   - Read views, customer progress marks and comments with audit.
10. **Surveys slice.** Definitions, triggers, send through dispatch, response intake, health components + reasons, play trigger.
    - Journey-moment surveys go into **one batch approval per day** (Q-30). Long-tail surveys may auto-send only under the auto-send guards.
    - Survey sends obey the **same frequency caps as any other outbound, even when batch-approved**: guard 1 (contact emailed < 3 days) and guard 2 (account emailed by another play < 48 h), plus suppression. A capped survey is rescheduled to the next eligible day, never dropped silently.
11. **Plays slice.** Waking the three dormant plays (`engagement-loss` on meeting/engagement data, `qbr-due`, and `business-review-follow-up`). For each, verify detector exists, safety check passes, and dormant label is updated/cleared.
12. **Seed + e2e slice.** `e2e/m10-meetings-qbr-portal.spec.ts` and axe on the new screens, including portal pages.

  - an expired token fails;
  - a revoked token fails;
  - a token for account A cannot read account B in the same workspace;
  - it cannot read another workspace;
  - a contact can only update their own customer-side steps.

- `src/modules/meetings/__tests__/summary-actions.test.ts` (RecordedProvider): action items → Tasks with owners; an unknown owner → unassigned task with a "No owner found" label, never an invented owner.
- `src/modules/surveys/__tests__/survey-health.test.ts`:
