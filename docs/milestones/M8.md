## 2. In scope
- **Models** ([W], RLS, composite FKs): `Play`, `PlayVersion`, `PlayRun` (unique `(workspaceId, playVersionId, accountId, triggerRef)`), `PlayStepRun`, `Campaign`, `CampaignRecipient`, `VoiceSample`.
- **`src/modules/plays/definition.ts`**: the Zod `PlayDefinition`.
  - `trigger`: `signal` (ruleKey) | `event` (outbox type) | `schedule` (cron) | `segmentEntry` (segmentId) | `noteTag` (tag).
  - `conditions` (field/op/value over Account, health band, ARR, renewal date, tier).
  - `branches` (by tier, health band, ARR band, renewal window).
  - `steps[]`: `{ key, handler: notify | task | draft | send | update | escalate | wait, params, approvalLevelByTier: { HIGH_TOUCH, MID_TOUCH, LONG_TAIL }, safe: boolean, sensitive: boolean }`.
  - `exit` conditions.
- **`src/modules/plays/safety.ts`** (pure, test-first). It refuses to publish, with plain-words reasons per refusal:
  1. the play does nothing (no step with an effect, or all branches empty);
  2. a missing required setting (Zod `params` incomplete, e.g. template missing for `draft`, assignee missing for `task`);
  3. a step whose `handler` has no registered implementation;
  4. a trigger that no enabled detector can produce: ruleKey not in the signal library or disabled, event type not emitted by any module, segment deleted. The message names the missing detector, e.g. "Trigger `CHAMPION_LEFT` needs the Champion Guard detector (arrives in M9)";
  5. `SEND_AUTOMATICALLY` on a step not marked `safe`, or on a `sensitive` step;
  6. unreachable steps or a branch condition that can never be true (simple static check).
- **Runner** (`src/modules/plays/runner.ts` + worker jobs `plays.enter`, `plays.step`):
  - idempotent entry (unique PlayRun key) and step execution keyed by `(playRunId, stepKey)`;
  - waits (`scheduledFor`), approval waits (`WAITING_APPROVAL` → resumes on `approval.decided`), exits;
  - the effective approval level = min(step level for tier, workspace ceiling, sensitive cap) per ARCH §7.3;
  - every customer email goes through `outbound.dispatch` only.
- **Auto-send enablement** (Admin only):
  - a per-play `autoSendEnabled` on PlayVersion steps marked `safe` (long-tail by default), plus the workspace master switch in `WorkspaceSettings`;
  - `sendMode=AUTO` messages pass `evaluateGuards` (built in M4). Any failing guard → `HELD` + an `Approval` with the guard reason; nothing is silently dropped;
  - enabling auto-send requires typed confirmation and writes an AuditLog row.
- **The 15 seeded plays** (`prisma/seed/plays/*.ts`, seeded as `DRAFT`, `isSeeded=true`), each with a realistic definition:
  1. Health Risk
  2. Renewal Risk
  3. Renewal Window (90/60/30)
  4. Onboarding Stall
  5. New Customer Handoff (uses M6 `handoff.ts`)
  6. Champion Loss
  7. Adoption Drop
  8. Expansion Opportunity
  9. Payment Failed
  10. Support Escalation / SLA Breach
  11. Promise Late
  12. QBR Due
  13. Engagement Loss / Re-engagement
  14. Cancellation Save (all customer steps `sensitive: true` → never auto)
  15. Business Review Follow-up

  **Champion Loss, Promise Late, QBR Due and Business Review Follow-up** cannot publish yet. The safety check shows the exact missing detector (M9: champion/promise rules; M10: QBR/meeting events). This is correct behaviour, not a stub.
- **Builder UI** (`/plays`, `/plays/[id]`):
  - list with status + results summary; inspect the definition in plain language;
  - edit trigger → conditions → steps; branch by tier/health/ARR/renewal;
  - per-step approval level per tier;
  - publish (runs the safety check), pause, kill;
  - version history.
- **Describe a play in plain words**: `play-builder` (deep tier, prompt `prompts/play-builder.md`).
  - The output is a `PlayDefinition` draft, then Zod parse, then the safety check, then a DRAFT `PlayVersion` for human review.
  - It never publishes itself, and it is audited as an AgentRun.
- **Play results** (`/plays/[id]/results`): accounts entered, actions taken, replies, saves, revenue (from OutcomeRecord + RevenueEvent; money via `redactFinancials`). "Dead plays" (no entries in 30 days, or a publishable trigger never fired) are flagged, with a Kill action.
- **Notes trigger a play**: the `noteTag` trigger, emitted by the `notes` module on `note.created`.
- **Agents**:
  - `outreach`: standard tier, prompt `prompts/outreach.md`.
    - It drafts emails/tasks for approved play steps in the company's voice, using `VoiceSample` (approved real sent examples from this workspace only, Rule 8) as in-prompt examples, never sent word-for-word.
    - It cites the account records its message refers to.
  - `digital-csm`: standard tier, prompt `prompts/digital-csm.md`. Limited form per spec §8:
    - it runs only the steps of seeded long-tail plays that are marked `safe`;
    - it hands off to a human (Task + Approval) the moment risk, anger, pricing, legal or a real opportunity appears (detected by the sensitive-topic detector, signals, and Extraction kinds);
    - autonomy tuning is Phase 2.
- **Campaigns** (`src/modules/campaigns/`, `/campaigns`):
  - segment audience (M2 segments) with a live count;
  - a simple editor (subject, body, merge fields from Account/Contact only);
  - **one Approval per campaign** (CS Leader/Admin);
  - send now or schedule;
  - pacing (`pacePerHour`, plus the workspace send rate);
  - every recipient goes through `outbound.dispatch` (suppression and guards 1, 3, 4, 5, 7 apply per recipient; held recipients are listed);
  - delivered/open/click/bounce via email provider webhooks (`POST /api/email/webhook`, signature verified);
  - an unsubscribe link in every message → `EmailSuppression`, honoured forever.
- **Setup wizard**: insert the "Proposed plays" step before Go live. It proposes which seeded plays to enable per tier from the workspace's data (deterministic rules: e.g. has billing integration → Payment Failed), and the human reviews them.
- **VoiceSample capture**: Settings › AI › Voice. An Admin selects real sent emails (from OutboundMessage SENT, or imported examples) as approved samples.

- `src/modules/outbound/__tests__/auto-send.int.test.ts`, through the runner, each guard → HELD + Approval:
  - contact emailed < 3 days (Orchard Lane);
  - another play emailed the account < 48 h;
  - quiet hours in contact time zone;
  - unknown time zone → hold;
  - open HIGH/URGENT or escalated ticket;
  - lifecycle LOST → never;
  - suppression;
  - master switch off.
  **A sensitive draft (cancel/pricing/legal/security/complaint/escalation) never auto-sends, even with auto-send on and all other guards passing.**

- `src/modules/agents/__tests__/outreach.int.test.ts`:
  - voice samples come only from the same workspace;
  - a draft citing an id outside the bundle → UNCITED;
  - a draft never bypasses dispatch.
- `src/modules/campaigns/__tests__/campaign.int.test.ts`:
  - no send without the campaign approval;
  - pacing respected (fake clock);
  - an unsubscribed contact is never sent to;
  - the unsubscribe link writes a suppression;
  - a bounce webhook updates the recipient and suppresses;
  - an invalid webhook signature is rejected.
- `src/modules/plays/__tests__/play-builder.int.test.ts` (RecordedProvider): the LLM draft is Zod-parsed and safety-checked, then saved as a DRAFT only.
