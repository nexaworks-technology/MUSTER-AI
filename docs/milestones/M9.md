## 1. Goal
Read calls, tickets and email for risk, goals, competitors and promises in the core product. Add an Inbox with honest drafted replies, the Promise Tracker, and sentiment & themes, with a verbatim quote as proof for every claim.

## 2. In scope
- **Models** (all [W], RLS + composite FKs):
  - `Extraction`, if M4 did not already create it. Verify against the schema and complete any missing `kind` values: SENTIMENT, COMPETITOR, BUDGET_CONCERN, PROMISE, GOAL, CANCEL_LANGUAGE, EXPORT_LANGUAGE, COMPLAINT, RENEWAL_CONCERN, ACTION_ITEM, DECISION, THEME, INTENT.
  - `Promise`.
  - `InboxItem`: accountId?, sourceType (MESSAGE | TICKET), sourceId, status (NEW | DRAFTED | REPLIED | CLOSED | UNMATCHED), intent, sentimentLabel, assigneeId, draftOutboundMessageId?, matchedBy (CONTACT_EMAIL | DOMAIN | MANUAL).
  - Complete `Goal` wiring for extraction-origin goals.
- **Adapters** (`src/modules/integrations/adapters/`):
  - `zendesk`, `freshdesk`: tickets + ticket comments → `NormalizedTicket` + `NormalizedMessage`, incremental cursor + webhooks.
  - `google-email` (Gmail API), `microsoft-email` (Graph): messages → `NormalizedMessage`. Read-only.
  - "Draft in mailbox" exists only via the write-back gate (INTERP-5).
  - Each adapter ships recorded-fixture contract tests.
- **Watcher (full).** The `watcher` agent's extraction step runs over every new Message, Ticket thread and CallTranscript chunk (≤ ~8k tokens).
  - Model tier: `fast`. Prompt: `prompts/watcher-extraction.md` (bump the version, with a changelog).
  - Idempotent per `(sourceType, sourceId, promptVersion)`. A backfill job `conversations.reextract` runs only on an explicit prompt-version bump.
- **Extraction validators** (`src/modules/conversations/extraction/validate.ts`):
  - every `quote` must be a verbatim substring of the source after whitespace normalization, otherwise the item is dropped and counted;
  - a per-kind payload Zod schema;
  - `PROMISE.dueDate` is parsed or null (never invented).
- **Text-based signal rules** (`src/modules/signals/rules/`): `competitor-mentioned`, `budget-concern`, `cancel-language`, `export-language`, `complaint-repeated` (≥ 2 COMPLAINT with the same THEME in 30d), `repeat-problem` (support), `angry-sentiment`, `renewal-concern`, `no-reply-important-followup`, `goal-stated` (→ `Goal`).
- **People rules**: `champion-silent`, `champion-bounced`, `champion-left`, `champion-role-change` (spec §6D People "role change"; a role change inside the same company is a known false-alarm cause, shown on the rule; `docs/REVIEW-2026-11-03.md` N1), `new-decision-maker`, `email-engagement-stops`.
- **Inbox** (`src/modules/inbox/`, route `/inbox`):
  - Inbound email or ticket → InboxItem on the right account. Match by contact email first, then domain; anything ambiguous or unknown goes to an **Unmatched** queue and is never auto-assigned.
  - Intent and sentiment come from Extraction (INTENT/SENTIMENT).
  - The Support Risk Agent drafts a reply that references what the customer actually wrote (cited message/ticket ids). The draft lands in Approvals via the M4 `outbound.dispatch` path.
- **Sensitive gate.** Any CANCEL_LANGUAGE / EXPORT_LANGUAGE / COMPLAINT / pricing / legal / security / escalation intent sets `Approval.isSensitive = true` and caps the level at DRAFT_FOR_APPROVAL. No auto-reply, ever.
- **Promise Tracker** (`src/modules/promises/`, route `/promises` plus an Account 360 tab):
  - Promises come from PROMISE extractions (with a quote) or from manual entry. Each has an owner and a due date; late promises sort first, then promises with no owner.
  - **Promise Keeper** (`prompts/promise-keeper.md`, tier `fast`) assigns owner/date suggestions and creates internal chase Tasks for late promises. It never contacts the customer.
  - **Sales handoff completed (carried from M6, Q-64):** `src/modules/onboarding/handoff.ts` (`assembleHandoff`) gains two item types it could not have before: the promises Sales made (from `Promise` rows, each with its source quote and link) and goals taken from calls (GOAL extractions → `Goal` rows with `origin AI`, quote, `agentRunId`). Every item carries `sources[]`; an item still missing says "Not found in CRM"; the M6 log-only line "Promise tracking starts in M9" goes away. The Account 360 › Onboarding handoff panel shows them with no other change.
- **Champion Guard** (`prompts/champion-guard.md`, tier `standard`): suggests the next-best contact from StakeholderRole/Message/Meeting data and drafts an intro (DRAFT_FOR_APPROVAL).
- **Support Risk Agent** (`prompts/support-risk.md`, tier `standard`): a risk narrative citing ticket ids and extractions, plus the Inbox reply draft.
- **Sentiment & themes** (`/health/sentiment` portfolio view plus an Account 360 Conversations panel). Theme counts and sentiment mix are computed deterministically from Extraction rows; every theme shows ≥ 1 quote with a source link. The Sentiment pillar is now fed by real SENTIMENT extractions (deterministic aggregation, ARCHITECTURE §4.1).
- **Plays.** Promise Late and Champion Loss now pass the M8 safety check and become publishable (they stay DRAFT until a human publishes).
- **Golden fixtures.** `prompts/__golden__/extraction/<kind>/*.json` for every kind (≥ 3 positive + ≥ 1 negative each). They replay in `pnpm test`; `pnpm eval` runs live outside the gate.

## 3. Explicitly out of scope

## 4. Build steps (dependency order; each slice ends with `pnpm gate` green)
1. **Schema slice.** Prisma models and migration for `Promise`, `InboxItem` and any missing `Extraction` kinds, with RLS + composite FKs. Extend the tenant suite. Gate.
2. **Validator slice (test-first).** Quote-substring validator, per-kind payload schemas, date parsing. Unit tests green.
3. **Watcher full slice.**
   - Extend the extraction job to Message / Ticket / CallTranscript, with chunking and idempotency key.
   - RecordedProvider replays the golden fixtures.
   - Add the backfill job.
4. **Text rules slice (test-first).** Each rule gets a fire and a not-fire fixture under `src/modules/signals/__tests__/fixtures/<ruleKey>/`. Register the rules and seed the `SignalRule` rows with the four spec §6D fields.
5. **People rules slice (test-first).** Same fixture discipline.
6. **Adapters slice.** Zendesk → Freshdesk → Gmail → Graph. Each one: contract tests on recorded payloads, webhook verify + dedupe via `WebhookEvent`, ingest via `ExternalRef`, plain-words `describeError`.
7. **Inbox slice.**
   - Matching service (test-first, incl. the ambiguous-domain case → UNMATCHED).
   - InboxItem creation on `message.received` / `ticket.opened`.
   - `/inbox` list with cursor pagination, plus the Unmatched queue with manual assign (audited).
8. **Support Risk Agent + reply drafts.** Agent definition, prompt and outputSchema citations. The draft becomes an OutboundMessage + Approval. The sensitive gate is wired to Extraction intents.
9. **Promise slice.** Promise service, PROMISE extraction → Promise upsert (dedupe on source + quote hash), Promise Keeper agent, chase tasks, `/promises` screen, Account 360 tab, `promise-late` + `promise-no-owner` signal rules, and the handoff items above (`handoff.test.ts` extended: promises and call goals each have sources, none invented, "Not found in CRM" when absent).
10. **Champion Guard slice.** Agent, next-best-contact ranking (deterministic candidates; the LLM only explains and drafts), and a Champion Guard card on Account 360.
11. **Sentiment & themes slice.** Aggregation queries (no N+1), screens, and the Sentiment pillar component switched on. Add a health re-score test.
12. **Plays slice.** Re-run the safety check: Promise Late and Champion Loss become publishable. Add a test.
13. **Seed + e2e slice.** Make sure the seed story data flows through. Write `e2e/m9-inbox-promises.spec.ts` and add the axe checks.
